Why Utter

Voice typing that cleans up after you.

Talking is about three times faster than typing. Utter turns how you talk into how you'd write, without sending a word to the cloud.

Works in every app

Text lands right at your cursor, in any app that takes typing. It stays on your clipboard too, so you can paste it again.

Smart cleanup

Removes ums and stutters, applies "actually, 3" corrections, turns spoken lists into numbered lists, and fixes punctuation.

Fits the app

Casual in Slack and WhatsApp, polished in Outlook and Gmail, untouched in code editors. Set your own rules per app.

Your words, your way

Add names and jargon so they're heard and spelled right. Save snippets: say "my email" and the whole address is typed.

100% offline

Whisper speech recognition and a small AI model run on your own PC. No account, no API keys, no internet.

Runs on older laptops

On first launch Utter checks your PC and picks the right models. Fast Mode keeps it quick on low-memory machines.

How to use

One shortcut. That's it.

Push to talk

For a sentence or two

  1. Click into any text box: an email, a chat, a doc.
  2. Hold Ctrl + Win and speak. A small amber bar at the bottom of the screen shows Utter is listening.
  3. Let go. The cleaned text is typed at your cursor and stays on your clipboard. Esc cancels.

Hands-free

For long messages and notes

  1. Hold Ctrl + Win, then tap Space. You can let go now.
  2. Talk as long as you like. Pause, think, carry on.
  3. Press Ctrl + Win again to finish. Change the shortcut anytime in Settings.

Questions

Does anything I say leave my PC?

No. Speech recognition (whisper.cpp) and the cleanup model (Qwen2.5 0.5B) run as local programs on your own computer. Utter has no account, no API keys and no cloud service. Your history is stored only on your PC and deleted after 30 days.

Is it like Wispr Flow?

It's the same idea, push-to-talk with smart cleanup, but free, open source and fully offline. Cloud apps can use bigger models, so their cleanup can be smarter. Utter trades that for privacy and zero cost.

Why is the download so big?

It includes two speech models and a small AI model, about 560 MB together, so Utter works with no internet from the first second. The app itself is about 8 MB.

It doesn't type into one app. Why?

Windows doesn't let normal apps type into apps running as administrator. If you need that, run Utter as administrator too. The text is still on your clipboard, so you can paste it with Ctrl+V.

Which languages work?

English for now. More languages are planned.

Mac or Linux?

Windows only for now. The code is built to be cross-platform, and Mac and Linux versions are planned.
