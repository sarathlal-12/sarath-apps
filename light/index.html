What's inside

Small island, lots of knobs.

Hover the island or press Alt+Space. Turn off anything you don't use in Look › Features.

Claude Code status

Know when a session needs your OK or has finished, with a soft pop. The Claude tab lists every session: Working (with a timer), Needs your OK, or Done.

Your phone on the island

Android or iPhone notifications through Phone Link, calls, a Copy button for login codes, and your phone's battery. Copy on the phone, paste on the PC.

Screenshots you can drag

Take a screenshot and a thumbnail pops up on the island. Drag it straight into a chat, a document or a folder.

Prompt snippets

Save the prompts you type again and again. One click pastes it into the app you were using.

Grab text or image

Ctrl+Alt+T copies the text in any part of the screen. Ctrl+Alt+S copies it as a picture. Read on your PC, nothing uploaded.

File shelf

Drop files on the island to hold them, then drag them out into any app or folder later.

Music and clipboard

Whatever's playing on your PC, with live sound waves, plus your last 50 copies: search, pin, drag any clip into any app, or pause the history.

Little moments

A green dot while the camera is on and an orange one for the mic, "Charging · 64%", finished downloads you can drag out, and a heads-up when a new .light is out.

Timer, notes, File lab

A focus timer that drains on the island, quick notes, and image conversion, resizing, cleaning and PDFs.

Fun switches

An island pet that follows your cursor, shy mode, typing fire, confetti, a rage-click detector, a CPU and GPU mood ring with temperatures, touch grass, night owl and a drink-water reminder. All off until you want them.

For coding with AI

Let Claude work. The island tells you when it needs you.

The island shows

  • "Needs your OK" when a session asks for permission
  • Which project is asking, and how many are waiting
  • A short "Done" with a soft pop when Claude finishes
  • A Claude tab: every session, Working with a timer, Needs your OK or Done

It stays quiet when

  • You're already looking at that terminal
  • A game or video is fullscreen
  • You switch it off in Look › Features
  • Nothing is sent anywhere: it all stays on your PC
Your phone

Your phone, on your PC's island.

Works with Android and iPhone through Phone Link, which comes with Windows. Nothing to buy, no extra app to make.

Notifications

The app and sender show on the island, like "WhatsApp · Mum". Switch on the message text if you want it. The Phone tab keeps the last 30, in memory only.

Codes and calls

A login code gets a Copy button, so you never type it again. Incoming calls show with the caller's name. Your phone's battery shows when it connects.

Copy on phone, paste on PC

Android: automatic, with Phone Link's cross-device copy and paste. iPhone: one tap with the free Send to .light Shortcut.

Install

No admin needed

  1. Download light-win-Setup.exe and run it. .light starts and appears in the system tray.
  2. Move your cursor to the top centre of the screen, or press Alt+Space.
  3. Open Look (the gear) to pick a light colour, island style, spot on screen and shortcuts.

Phone notifications

Android or iPhone, optional

  1. Open Phone Link (it comes with Windows) and pair your phone. Android uses the Link to Windows app; iPhone pairs over Bluetooth.
  2. In Phone Link's settings, turn on notifications and leave it running.
  3. Notifications show on the island and in .light's Phone tab. Login codes get a Copy button.
  4. Android copy and paste: turn on Cross-device copy and paste in Phone Link. Phone copies land in your clipboard.

iPhone clipboard

One tap with an Apple Shortcut

  1. In .light's Phone tab, open the clipboard page and turn on iPhone clipboard. When Windows asks, allow Private networks.
  2. On your iPhone, get the Send to .light Shortcut. When you add it, it asks for the Address, then the Code that .light shows.
  3. Copy something and run it. Keep it one tap away in Control Center, on the Action Button or with Back Tap. Phone and PC need the same Wi-Fi.

Claude Code status

Optional

  1. Open .light, then Look › Features, and turn on Claude Code status.
  2. .light adds a small hook to Claude Code's settings and keeps a backup. Turning it off removes it again.
  3. Start a new Claude Code session. The island lights up when it needs you.

Questions

Does it get in the way of games or videos?

No. When something fills the screen, the island fades away and stops taking clicks. It comes back when you leave fullscreen.

Does Grab text send my screen anywhere?

No. It uses the text recognition built into Windows, on your PC. Nothing is uploaded.

What does the Claude Code hook change?

It adds entries for "Notification", "Stop", "UserPromptSubmit" and "SessionEnd" to Claude Code's settings.json, and saves a backup copy first. Your other settings and hooks stay as they are.

Can I drag a screenshot into another app?

Yes. After a screenshot, drag the thumbnail off the island, or drag any picture or text out of the clipboard list. Explorer and chat apps get a PNG file; Paint, Word and editors get the picture itself.

How do the camera and mic dots work?

.light reads what Windows already knows: which apps are recording from a microphone right now, and which are using the camera. It only reads; it never turns anything on or off. On some recent Windows builds the camera record isn't kept, so only the mic dot shows.

Why do I see a phone notification twice?

Windows shows its own banner too. In Phone Link, open Settings › Features › Notifications and turn off "Show notification banners". Keep notifications on, or .light won't see them either.

Can I stop the player popping up for every YouTube Short?

It already stays quiet while you swipe: the now-playing popup shows once, then waits until things settle. To turn it off completely, use Look › Features › Now playing popup.

How do I update .light?

When a new version is out, the island tells you once with an Update button. You can also download the newest installer from this page and run it; your settings, clips and notes stay.

Do my phone notifications leave my PC?

No. Phone Link brings them to Windows, and .light only reads what Windows already has. The Phone tab keeps them in memory and forgets them when .light closes. By default the island shows only the app and sender, not the message.

Why does the iPhone clipboard need a tap?

iPhones don't let other devices read their clipboard in the background. A one-tap Apple Shortcut sends what you copied to .light over your Wi-Fi. .light only accepts it from your own network with your pairing code.

Does the shelf copy my files?

No, it only remembers where they are. Dragging a file out of the shelf copies it to wherever you drop it.

Can I move the island?

Yes. Pick one of eight spots in Look, or drag the open island by its top bar. On the left and right the island stands upright.
